At 30: how many weeks remain, and how much is true free time?

An 80-year life is 4,160 weeks: at age 30, 1,560 weeks are lived and 2,600 weeks (50 summers) remain. At 4.0 hr/day of screen time, 8.3 years go to screens versus 8.3 years of true free time (cutting 2 hr/day reclaims +4.2 free years).

What is a Life in Weeks (Memento Mori) calendar?

An 80-year human lifespan equals 4,160 weeks. Laying out every week in rows of 52 makes finite time concrete and reveals where your remaining waking hours actually go.

How many years of life does daily phone screen time take?

At 4.5 hours of daily phone and leisure screen time, roughly 27.3% of a 16.5-hour waking day goes to screens. For a 28-year-old with 52 years remaining, that adds up to 9.8 uninterrupted years looking at a display.
