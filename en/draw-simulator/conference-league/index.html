How does the Conference League Knockout Play-off draw work?

Clubs finishing 1st–8th advance directly to the Round of 16. Teams ranked 9th–24th enter two-legged play-offs paired by rank: 9/10 vs 23/24, 11/12 vs 21/22, 13/14 vs 19/20, and 15/16 vs 17/18 via a 50/50 draw, with 9th–16th hosting the second leg.

Can teams from the same country face each other in the Play-offs?

Yes. Under UEFA's 2024–27 format there is no country protection in the knockout phase, and clubs that already met during the league phase can also be drawn together.
