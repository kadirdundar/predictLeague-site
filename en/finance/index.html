How does Spend It All work?

In Get Rich Mode you pick a billionaire's fortune and buy items from everyday gadgets to private jets, mega yachts and NBA franchises until you hit zero. In Future Me Mode you simulate your own monthly compound returns and spend the projected portfolio.

How big is $1 billion in $100 bills?

$1 billion in $100 bills is 10 million banknotes weighing 10 tonnes and forming a 2.24 m cube — taller than a 1.75 m person.
