❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Who leads the Europa League table, and who is most likely to qualify?

After 54 matches Benfica lead with 9 points. The model's likeliest top-8 finishers: Benfica (96%), Bayer Leverkusen (92%), Bournemouth (76%).

How does the Europa League 2026/27 league phase work?

36 teams play in one table; each plays 8 matches (4 home, 4 away). The top 8 go straight to the round of 16, 9th to 24th play a knockout play-off and 25th to 36th are out.

How many points are needed for the top 8 in the Europa League?

In the UEFA Europa League league phase (8 matches, at most 24 points) 16 points are enough for the top 8 and 10 points for the play-off places (9-24) 90% of the time. 15 points give about an even chance of the top 8, 9 of the top 24. Source: 4,000 simulated league phases of the PredictLeague model, played matches included (2026-10-31).
