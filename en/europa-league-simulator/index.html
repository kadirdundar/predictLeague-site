❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Europa League 2026/27 Simulator work?

Pick your team or enter match scores for any matchday in the 36-team UEFA Europa League league phase. Our Monte Carlo engine keeps your predictions fixed, simulates every remaining match 1,000 times using Poisson goal ratings, and calculates exact Top 8, Play-off and Elimination probabilities.

How many points are needed for Top 8 and Play-offs in the Europa League?

In the UEFA Europa League league phase (8 matches, at most 24 points) 16 points are enough for the top 8 and 11 points for the play-off places (9-24) 90% of the time. 15 points give about an even chance of the top 8, 10 of the top 24. Source: 4,000 simulated league phases of the PredictLeague model, played matches included (2026-11-26).
