How much does €1 billion weigh and how big is it?

€1 billion in €200 notes is 5 million pieces weighing 5.5 tonnes; packed tightly it forms a 1.92 m cube (161 cabin suitcases). Fills 16 car trunks, or 23% of a 12 m² room. Stacked flat, it reaches 600 m high.

How is it calculated?

The amount is divided by the largest note ($100, €200) or 1 kg gold bar, then multiplied by one piece's exact volume and weight (ignoring air gaps and straps). Cabin suitcase: 55 × 40 × 20 cm; car trunk: 450 L. €200 note (Europa series): 153 × 77 × 0.12 mm, 1.1 g (OeNB).
